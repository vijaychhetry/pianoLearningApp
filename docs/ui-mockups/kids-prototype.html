Kids Piano 0.6.0 — level ladder, songs, and the falling-notes game

Five white keys (C4–G4) are the whole vocabulary. The ladder starts on one key, adds a second, jumbles those two, then adds E, F and G one at a time. Every press is answered on screen — including the key the child actually hit, ringed in amber. Sessions end with a real tune playable on the keys learned so far. The falling-notes game is the reward at the top, with a wide hit window and no way to lose. Screens below are prototypes, not the installed app. Levels 1–7 and the songs ship as 0.6.0; the falling game is level 8 and ships as 0.7.0, once the real microphone-to-judgement delay has been measured from an exported session log.
